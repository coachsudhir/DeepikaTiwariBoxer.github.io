About the Athlete

Deepika Tiwari is a professional boxer from Varanasi, India, competing in the light flyweight category. With the title of Youth World Champion (2019), she represents a new generation of Indian female athletes committed to competitive excellence at national and international levels. Her career combines discipline, technical skill, and a health-first approach to performance, making her an ideal partner for brands aligned with fitness, sports, and athlete development.

Key Achievements

  • Youth World Champion (2019) – Major international title achievement
  • Professional Debut Victory (2016) – Defeated Pinki Mondal (Unanimous Decision)
  • International Competition (2018) – Battle of Butuan II, Philippines
  • Light Flyweight Competitive Category – Consistent professional-level performance, Rank#1 (INDIA)
  • Military Recognition (2019) – Major General Gagan Deep Bakshi, SM, VSM acknowledgment
  • GEC Global Excellence Award (2025) – Recognition for consistency and sportsmanship

Career Overview

Foundation & Training: Trained under Coach Dharmendra Chauhan, developing technical boxing fundamentals and discipline. Advanced training with Coach Janardan Yadav focused on tactical precision and mental conditioning at Bob Gym in Varanasi.

Professional Path: Professional debut in 2016 with an impressive victory. Competed internationally in the Philippines (2018). Achieved Youth World Champion status in 2019, representing India at competitive levels.

Mentorship & Support: Supported by Dr. Pradeep 'Baba' Madhok, former World Bodybuilding Federation President, providing world-class training facilities and expertise.

Current Focus: Preparing for upcoming international bouts with emphasis on championship-level performance, recovery systems, and competitive readiness.
